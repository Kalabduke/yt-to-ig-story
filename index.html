YouTube → Instagram Story

Paste a YouTube link and get a 1080×1920 story card: cover art, title, channel and a link sticker. On a phone, Share hands the card straight to the Instagram app.

Both save the card to your photos first, then open Instagram — pick the newest image in the gallery thumb. Instagram shows its own picker, so Story lets you post and DM lets you choose who it goes to.

Tip: install this page from your browser menu (Add to home screen), then share a link straight from the YouTube app into it — the card comes back ready to post.

On iPhone/iPad: tap Share → Add to Home Screen to keep it next to Instagram. Sharing the card out to Instagram works from here; iOS cannot send a YouTube link into this app (that is an Android feature), so paste the link above.
